# Aegis

## AI Disaster Early-Action Platform

Aegis is an AI-powered disaster intelligence platform designed to provide localized disaster-risk information and early-action guidance.

The initial version focuses on flood and extreme-rainfall risk.

## Project Goals

- Analyze weather and rainfall data
- Detect unusual weather patterns
- Estimate localized disaster risk
- Analyze potentially affected areas
- Provide understandable risk explanations
- Provide preparedness guidance
- Provide a conversational AI interface

## Important Note

Aegis is intended as a decision-support and educational system. AI-generated predictions do not replace official emergency warnings or instructions from relevant authorities.
