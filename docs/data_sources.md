# Aegis Data Sources

## 1. ERA5

Provider:
Copernicus Climate Change Service

Purpose:
Historical global weather and atmospheric data.

Potential variables:
- precipitation
- temperature
- humidity
- pressure
- wind

Use:
Weather feature generation and historical analysis.

---

## 2. NASA IMERG

Provider:
NASA Global Precipitation Measurement Mission

Purpose:
Global precipitation observations/estimates.

Use:
Precipitation analysis and potential near-real-time data.

---

## 3. Global Flood Database

Purpose:
Historical flood events and flood extent.

Use:
Creating flood-event labels and validating the ML model.

---

## 4. Global Human Settlement Layer

Provider:
Copernicus

Purpose:
Population and settlement information.

Use:
Exposure and impact analysis.

---

## 5. NOAA IBTrACS

Provider:
NOAA

Purpose:
Historical tropical cyclone information.

Use:
Future multi-hazard expansion.
